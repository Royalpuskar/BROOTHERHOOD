SUGGESTED CLASSROOM USE

One lesson. Many ways to learn.

Start by asking students to predict where oxygen-poor blood goes. Use the 3D model or the text list to compare the right and left sides. Then trace the two routes and use the quiz as a quick knowledge check.

Accessible by design: reduced-motion mode, descriptive text, keyboard-accessible anatomy buttons, and the same lesson content when 3D rendering is unavailable.

Accuracy note: the model is intentionally schematic and should accompany a standard anatomy diagram when more precise spatial relationships are required.

Learning goals

  • Identify the four chambers and explain their roles.
  • Recognize the tricuspid, pulmonary, mitral and aortic valves.
  • Trace blood between the body, heart and lungs.
  • Explain why arteries and veins are named for flow direction.
